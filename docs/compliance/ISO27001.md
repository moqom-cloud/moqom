# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 mapping and Statement of Applicability

Controls designed to align with ISO/IEC 27001:2022. **MOQOM is not certified**
and no certification body has been engaged. Certification is planned after a
SOC 2 Type I, because most of the evidence is shared. Control IDs in the last
columns refer to the [catalog](README.md#control-catalog).

| Clause | Requirement | MOQOM | Status |

| 10 | Nonconformity, corrective action, improvement | Findings → register → commit that fixes → next report shows resolved | Planned (code ready) |
